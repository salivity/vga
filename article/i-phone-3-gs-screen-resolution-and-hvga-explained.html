iPhone 3GS Screen Resolution and HVGA Explained

This article examines the mathematical relationship between the Apple iPhone 3GS display resolution and the Half Video Graphics Array (HVGA) standard. It breaks down the exact pixel counts, linear dimensions, and aspect ratio transformations that define both display formats, demonstrating how the iPhone 3GS mathematically implements the HVGA specification.

The Apple iPhone 3GS features a display resolution of 320 × 480 pixels in portrait orientation (or 480 × 320 pixels in landscape orientation). This exact resolution is the standard definition of Half Video Graphics Array (HVGA).

To understand the mathematical relationship, one must first look at the standard Video Graphics Array (VGA) baseline. A standard VGA display has a resolution of 640 × 480 pixels. Multiplying these dimensions yields the total pixel count of VGA:

640 × 480 = 307,200 pixels

HVGA literally means "Half VGA." Mathematically, it represents exactly 50 percent of the total pixel volume of a standard VGA display:

307,200 pixels ÷ 2 = 153,600 pixels

The iPhone 3GS achieves this precise total pixel volume:

320 × 480 = 153,600 pixels

Linear scaling explains how these dimensions are calculated. Rather than scaling both dimensions down by the square root of two, HVGA divides only one axis of the standard VGA grid by two while leaving the other axis unaltered:

  • Horizontal axis: 640 ÷ 2 = 320 pixels
  • Vertical axis: 480 × 1 = 480 pixels

This dimensional change also alters the aspect ratio. Standard VGA features a 4:3 aspect ratio (640:480 reduces to 4:3, or approximately 1.33:1). Because one axis is halved relative to the other, the aspect ratio changes:

(4 ÷ 2) : 3 = 2:3 (or 3:2 in landscape orientation)

Expressed as a decimal, 480 ÷ 320 equals 1.5:1.

Therefore, the Apple iPhone 3GS display is a direct mathematical implementation of the HVGA standard, containing exactly half the total pixels of a VGA display and maintaining a 3:2 aspect ratio derived from halving a single axis of the original 4:3 VGA grid.
