VGA 640x480 Horizontal Sync Pulse Duration

In standard 640x480 Video Graphics Array (VGA) timing at a 60 Hz refresh rate, the duration of the horizontal synchronization (H-sync) pulse is exactly 96 pixel clock cycles, which corresponds to approximately 3.813 microseconds. This article breaks down the exact timing calculations, the pixel clock frequency, and the surrounding horizontal timing parameters required to generate a compliant standard VGA video signal.

Standard Pixel Clock and Timing Math

The industry-standard timing specification for 640x480 VGA running at a 59.94 Hz (commonly referred to as 60 Hz) refresh rate is defined by the VESA standard. This specification relies on a base pixel clock frequency of 25.175 MHz.

At 25.175 MHz, the time period of a single pixel clock cycle (\(T_{pixel}\)) is calculated as:

\[T_{pixel} = \frac{1}{25,175,000 \text{ Hz}} \approx 39.722 \text{ nanoseconds}\]

Because the standard allocates exactly 96 pixel clock periods to the horizontal sync pulse:

\[\text{Duration} = 96 \times 39.722 \text{ ns} \approx 3.813 \text{ microseconds}\]

Complete Horizontal Line Profile

To understand where the sync pulse fits into the overall scanline, the total horizontal period consists of 800 total pixel clocks. The breakdown of a single horizontal line is as follows:

  • Active Video (Visible Area): 640 pixels (\(25.422 \ \mu\text{s}\))
  • Front Porch: 16 pixels (\(0.636 \ \mu\text{s}\))
  • Horizontal Sync Pulse: 96 pixels (\(3.813 \ \mu\text{s}\))
  • Back Porch: 48 pixels (\(1.907 \ \mu\text{s}\))
  • Total Line Period: 800 pixels (\(31.778 \ \mu\text{s}\))

A full line duration of 31.778 microseconds results in a horizontal line frequency of approximately 31.469 kHz.

Signal Characteristics

For standard 640x480 @ 60 Hz, the horizontal synchronization pulse uses negative polarity. This means the signal remains high (logic high, typically 3.3V or 5V depending on the controller interface) during the visible area and porch regions, and pulls low (logic low / 0V) for the duration of the 3.813-microsecond sync period to signal the display to reset the horizontal raster scan to the beginning of the next line.
