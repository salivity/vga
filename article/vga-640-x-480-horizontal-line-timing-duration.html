VGA 640x480 Horizontal Line Timing Duration

In standard 640x480 Video Graphics Array (VGA) timing running at a 60 Hz refresh rate, the duration of a single horizontal line is precisely 31.778 microseconds (µs). This measurement is derived from the standard 25.175 MHz pixel clock operating across a total line width of 800 pixel periods. This article explains the exact mathematics, horizontal scan frequency, and specific segment-by-segment timing breakdowns that define a complete VGA scan line.

The Horizontal Scan Duration Calculation

The timing of a standard 640x480 @ 60 Hz VGA display is governed by a pixel clock frequency of 25.175 MHz (25,175,000 cycles per second). Although the visible portion of the screen displays only 640 pixels across, each line requires additional non-visible pixel clock cycles for synchronization and blanking intervals.

A full horizontal line consists of 800 total pixel intervals. The duration (\(T_H\)) of one horizontal line is calculated by dividing the total number of pixels per line by the pixel clock frequency:

\[T_H = \frac{800 \text{ pixels}}{25,175,000 \text{ Hz}} \approx 0.00003177756 \text{ seconds} \approx 31.778 \text{ }\mu\text{s}\]

This corresponds to a horizontal line frequency (\(f_H\)) of:

\[f_H = \frac{25.175 \text{ MHz}}{800} \approx 31.46875 \text{ kHz}\]

Timing Breakdown of a Single Horizontal Line

A single 31.778 µs horizontal line is divided into four distinct phases:

  • Active Display (Visible Video): 640 pixels
    • Duration: 25.422 µs
    • The period during which color signals (RGB) are driven to create visible pixels on the display.
  • Front Porch: 16 pixels
    • Duration: 0.636 µs
    • A brief blanking period after the active display area where video signals are pulled to blank level before the sync pulse begins.
  • Horizontal Sync Pulse (HSync): 96 pixels
    • Duration: 3.813 µs
    • A negative-polarity pulse that signals the display hardware to reset the horizontal scan position back to the left side of the screen.
  • Back Porch: 48 pixels
    • Duration: 1.907 µs
    • A stabilization blanking period following the sync pulse before active pixels begin drawing again.

Summing these individual sections produces the total 800 pixel clocks, equating directly to the 31.778 microsecond standard horizontal line duration.
